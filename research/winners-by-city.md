# GLOBAL SYNTHESIS (~30 known winners)
1. **Selection / decision layers over agents** — openCortex (SF 1st), Hyperreasoning (JetBrains 1st), Paradigm, Jevolution. ← Decisions API is literally this.
2. **Agents that finish a whole real job, with guardrails** — incident response (Tokyo 1st), negotiate-and-buy (Taipei 1st TurnDeal), **agent payments within limits (LatAm 1st AgentPay)**, freight procurement, care coordination (Taipei 3rd), camera → alerts → Telegram (Pune 1st Air Secure).
3. **Security / trust** — Sentinel, Codex-Astartes, AttenuAI, SecureLoop, RedShield, SentinelOps.
4. **Care / accessibility for a named user** — Pulse, veTriage, Second Voice, ことばリンク, 長照 Agent.
5. **Games / multi-agent sims** — Singapore 1st, Taipei 2nd, Seoul (all), Berlin hobo.berlin.
Common: concrete user; human-in-the-loop or deterministic checks; delivered where people already are (Telegram, IDE, payment flow); complete working demo; explicit Codex process (Skills, AGENTS.md, no human code).
